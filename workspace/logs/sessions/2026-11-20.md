## Rhyme canvas (user idea, no separate order file)

- **Request:** a Google-Maps-like dark canvas below the Reimarchiv list, with vowel tables (A/E/I/O/U) as movable windows.
- **Built:**
  - `frontend/dist/views/canvas.js`: pan, zoom at the cursor, fit, windows (move, resize, z-order, close), splitter between list and canvas.
  - The Reimarchiv page now fills the window height (`fill` flag on views).

- **Verified in the real app:**
  - Via UI Automation: opening windows A/I/U, "Einpassen".
  - Via window messages sent to the WebView: dragging a window header (moved exactly by the drag distance) and panning the background.
  - Persistence: the state was read from the database after closing, and the layout was restored after a restart.
- **Not verified by script:** wheel zoom. Posted wheel messages are ignored by WebView2. During the test, zoom changes did arrive from a real wheel or touchpad over the app window.
